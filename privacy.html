Privacy Policy

Effective March 1, 2026

Falling Notes does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All user content is stored locally on your device.

When you use the transcription or color generation features, your audio, video, or a thumbnail is sent to a third-party service for processing. No identifying information is included in these requests.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
